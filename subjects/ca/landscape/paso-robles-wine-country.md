# Paso Robles wine country (landscape unit): research notes

Phase P5, writer session 2026-10-05. 4 cards, before the §9 check. Unit id
`paso-robles-wine-country`. Written as a landscape unit rather than a sight because the
vineyards line the route from east of Paso Robles to Adelaida, and the official sources give a
district-by-district picture. `near`: the region-wide cards use the *Paso Robles AVA* article's
coordinates (35.6071502, -120.6081466) at 30,000 m; the Highway 46 card uses the same point at
12,000 m; the Adelaida District card uses Adelaida (Wikipedia) at 10,000 m.

## Cards
- since-1983: AVA established 4 October 1983 (eCFR history note: T.D. ATF-148, 48 FR 45241); the
  boundary starts where SLO, Kings and Kern counties meet and runs west along the county line;
  commercial winemaking began in the 1880s with Ascension (York Mountain); the wine festival began
  in 1983; wine tourism grew in the 1990s.
- hot-days-cold-nights: July average high 93.2 °F at Paso Robles airport (facts file, NOAA
  1991-2020); the growing-season day-night swing across the AVA is 20 to 50 degrees, and over 50 in
  the Highlands District (TTB table in the 2014 final rule); the climate is maritime, becoming more
  continental to the east.
- highway-46-line: CA-46 east of town is part of the boundary between the Estrella and Geneseo
  Districts (2014 boundary descriptions); there are eleven districts (2014); the landforms of each
  (TTB table); California's conjunctive labeling law.
- adelaida-district: Chimney Rock Road and Nacimiento Lake Drive form part of the Adelaida District
  boundary (§ 9.238 text: "San Miguel Road (locally known as Chimney Rock Road, then Nacimiento Lake
  Drive ..."); 900 to 2,200 feet; light sea breezes and little fog; thin lime-rich soils on bedrock.

## Numbers: official only
Every figure is from the Federal Register or eCFR (TTB/ATF rules), or from the facts file (NOAA). No
winery counts are used: "more than 200 wineries" appears in Wikipedia and Travel Paso, but there is no
official table for it. Acreage figures (609,673 acres in 1983, 669,253 later; 53,000 acres for
Adelaida; 18,500 and 5,000 vineyard acres) are left out: some come from Wikipedia prose, and they
conflict.

## Conflicts and weak spots
- Wikipedia says grapes came to the area "in 1787 by Spanish missionaries at Mission San Miguel
  Arcangel, founded in 1797". It contradicts itself, so the mission-grape sentence was dropped.
- "Zinfandel was introduced to the area" in Paderewski's vineyard is a "first" with one source, so
  it is not stated as a first; the Paderewski town card only says he planted it.
- "44th AVA in the nation" is a single-source ranking; not used.
- Which side of CA-46 each district lies on is not stated plainly in the boundary text. The card says
  only that the road forms part of the line between them.
