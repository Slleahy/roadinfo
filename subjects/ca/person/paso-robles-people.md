# Paso Robles and Adelaida people (person): research notes

Phase P5, writer session 2026-10-05. 1 card, before the §9 check.

## The bar as applied
Candidates came from the "Notable people" list in Wikipedia's *Paso Robles, California* (rev
1377211382). §3a requires that a listener would recognise the name, and that the person was born
here, raised here or did the notable work here.

## In (1 card)
- **Josh Brolin**: raised here. His article: "Brolin was raised on a ranch in the Adelaida area of Paso
  Robles, attending elementary and middle school in Templeton". He clears recognition easily (Thanos,
  *No Country for Old Men*, *The Goonies*, an Oscar nomination for *Milk*). Because the tie is to the
  Adelaida area, the card is anchored on Adelaida (`{"type": "place", "name": "Adelaida"}`, `near`
  at Adelaida, 8,000 m), where the route ends. He is living, so the card covers only his roles,
  his schooling and the move to Santa Barbara at about 11. Left out: his marriages, family and
  everything personal. His *Milk* role (Dan White) is named only as the film title.
  Fetches: 1 of 5 (Wikipedia *Josh Brolin*, rev 1375644488).

## Out, and why
- **Ignacy Jan Paderewski**: world-famous, but his notable work (the piano, Poland's government)
  was not done here. He owned ranches and planted vines here, which is "owned property here". He is
  told instead in the town card `ca-town-paso-robles-paderewski-01`, which is about the place.
- **King Vidor**, film director: died at his Paso Robles ranch. "Died here" is ruled out.
- **Elena Verdugo**, actress, born in Paso Robles. A real tie, but few listeners in 2026 would know
  her name (*Marcus Welby, M.D.*), and nothing fetched gives a hook strong enough on its own. Out on
  recognition. Reconsider only if the owner wants a second Paso Robles person.
- **Hunter Tylo, Casey Biggs, Dennis Harrah, Jake Zemke, Frank Armitage**: "lived here", "resident"
  or "retired here". Not a tie.
- **Terry Hoage**: owned a winery here. That is property, and he is below the recognition bar.
- **Hal Rhyne, Jason Botts, Frank Minini, Dylan Beavers, Derrick Jasper, Bailey Gaither, Mitchell Van
  Dyk, Josh Oliver, Don Parish, Hamp Pool, Larry Grant, Rusty Kuntz**: born or schooled here, but
  below the recognition bar. These are the "dead air with a name in it" §3a warns about.
- **Drury James**, a founder of the town: below the bar as a person, but his link to Jesse James is the
  story. Told in the town card `ca-town-paso-robles-jesse-james-uncle-01`.
