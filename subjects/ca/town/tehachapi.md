## Sources found
- `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehachapi,_California` — name meaning, elevation, agriculture,
  viticultural area, prison, the Double Indemnity line.
- `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehichipa,_California` — the original town, James Williams, the
  1876 bypass, the Errea House. Pairs with California Historical Landmark 643 (Old Town) on the
  Office of Historic Preservation Kern County page, `https://ohp.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=21423`.
- `https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=610` — Tomo-Kahni, guided tour only.
- `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehachapi_Railroad_Depot` — 1904 build, National Register 1999,
  2008 fire, 2009 rebuild.
- `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Correctional_Institution` — 1932 women's institution,
  closed after the 1952 quake, reopened 1954.

## Open questions and conflicts
- **Where the original town stood.** Wikipedia's Tehachapi article says the first settlement was
  "about four miles west of present downtown"; the Tehichipa article gives coordinates
  35.0858, -118.2085, which is well east of town, near Monolith. The cards say only that the
  railroad bypassed the old place; they do not say where it was. Needs a local-history source.
- **Earthquake magnitude.** Wikipedia and USGS publication 70021492 both give Mw 7.3; the Caltech
  data centre page gives Mw 7.5, and a 2018 reanalysis put published estimates at Mw 7.2 to 7.5.
  The town card avoids the number; only the landscape White Wolf card states 7.3, with two sources.
- **Trains per day** is quoted differently everywhere: 36 (ASCE, and Wikipedia's 20 plus 16),
  30 to 40 (American-Rails), about 50 (Tehachapi News). The cards use 36, which two sources agree on.
- No `checked` field on any card, per instruction; the checking pass sets it.

## Leads not followed (budget or sourcing)
- The city's own history page was never fetched. Likely the best source for the Williamsburg to
  Greenwich to Tehachapi name sequence that landmark 643 hints at.
- **Little Feat's "Willin'"** names Tehachapi in the lyric and is a genuinely good card for the ear.
  The Wikipedia fetch only returned that the song was written by Lowell George, not the lyric. Needs
  a source that quotes the line.
- Gliding and soaring: Wikipedia says only "excellent air conditions for gliding". Mountain Valley
  Airport and the soaring operation there would make a better card with a real source.
- Tehachapi Mountains American Viticultural Area: the acreage (about 58,000) came from a search
  summary, not a table. The Treasury alcohol bureau's final rule in the Federal Register is the
  right source and would also give the boundary and the date.
- Film and television shot here: Star Trek Generations and others, per Wikipedia. Not sourced.
- The 1932 women's institution has a rich history (it was California's first) that one card only
  gestures at. The state corrections department's own history page was not fetched.
- Population: the facts file holds Kern County only. Tehachapi's 2020 census count is 12,939 and
  the 2024 estimate 11,114 per Wikipedia's table, but no population card was written because the
  prison population distorts the figure and that needs explaining with a source.

## Checking pass, 2026-10-04
- **The Tehachapi etymology was dropped from the Tomo-Kahni card.** The Wikipedia article gives the
  Kawaiisu "Tihachipia / hard climb" derivation *and* a competing one from Yokuts *taheech[e]*
  ("oak-covered flat") plus Ute *pah'* ("water"). One source, two explanations, so stating "it means
  hard climb" as settled fact fails. If a later phase finds a second source backing the Kawaiisu
  derivation, it is a good card on its own.
- **Tomo-Kahni is not in town.** The state parks page says tours start at the Tehachapi Museum and
  then drive "approximately 12 miles" to the site, and the park's Wikipedia coordinates
  (35.23556, -118.58333) are about eleven miles from the centre of town. The card said the village
  was "here", which fails the ear pass from CA-58; it now carries `{distance}` and an `elsewhere`
  block.
- **"Opened on this site in 1932" was wrong for the listener.** CCI is at 35.111, -118.569, in
  Cummings Valley, about seven miles from the town centre the card plays at. "On this site" came out.
- **California's first women's prison: confirmed, second source added.** Kathleen A. Cairns,
  *Hard Time at Tehachapi: California's First Women's Prison* (University of New Mexico Press, 2009)
  is independent of Wikipedia and carries the claim in its title. Note the review of it says the
  place was "not the first all-female prison" — that reads as first-in-the-nation, not
  first-in-California, but a checker should know the sentence exists. The CDCR's own history page
  (insidecdcr.ca.gov) would settle it; the domain would not resolve on this run.
- **Apples and wine card.** "Higher than the San Joaquin Valley on one side and the Mojave Desert on
  the other" was an inference — the source only says the city lies *between* them. Reworded.
  "Has always been farming country" softened to "has long been", which is what "historically
  agrarian" supports.
- Everything else in the Tehachapi deck checked out verbatim: the depot's 1904 build, the
  20 October 1999 Register listing, the 2008 fire and 2009 rebuild; the whole Tehichipa /
  Williamsburg / Errea House sequence; the Double Indemnity line; the 1952 damage counts and the
  epicentre coordinates (34°52′N 119°06′W, which is what the card's `elsewhere` block holds).
