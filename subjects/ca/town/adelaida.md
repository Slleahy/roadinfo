# Adelaida (town): research notes

Phase P5, writer session 2026-10-05. 2 town cards, before the §9 check, plus the Josh Brolin
`person` card (see `subjects/ca/person/paso-robles-people.md`). The owner's route says "Adelaide".
The Wikipedia article is *Adelaida, California*: "pronounced as, and in the 19th century commonly
spelled as Adelaide". The cards use the modern spelling. Every card naming Adelaida carries
`pronunciations` {"Adelaida": "Adelaide"} on that source's authority. `near` 35.64555556,
-120.87361111 (Wikipedia), radius 8,000 m.

## Cards
- mining-town: a mercury-mining town in the 1880s (Klau and Buena Vista); community center, saloon,
  dance hall, general store; post office 1877–1936; businesses moved to Paso Robles; the mines ran into
  the 1970s (EPA: 1868–1970); the 1917 schoolhouse on Chimney Rock Road, open until 1964, and the
  cemetery are what is left.
- mercury-in-the-creek: the mines on a ridge of the Santa Lucia Range; mercury into Las Tablas Creek and
  Lake Nacimiento; channel cleanup in 2000; Superfund listing on 19 April 2006; state fish advisories.
