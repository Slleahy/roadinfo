## Where it is
35.656178, -120.378817 (Wikipedia). `near` radius 8,000 m. SR 46 and SR 41 join here and share the
road northeast to Cholame.

## Cards
- chapel-hill: William P. Clark Jr.'s mission-style chapel on the hill above his ranch, built after
  his 1988 plane crash; ceilings and features bought from the Hearst Corporation; private land,
  Sunday Mass and concerts. Clark was born in Oxnard, so he is not a `person` card here (§3a).
- where-the-estrella-begins: San Juan Creek and Cholame Creek meet to form the Estrella River;
  Cholame Valley between the Diablo Range and the Cholame Hills; SR 46 follows the river; 41 and 46
  share the road to Cholame.

## Gaps
- **The founding.** Search snippets (from the HMDB "Shandon" marker, which this fetcher cannot open)
  say pioneer families settled Starkey Flats near the creek junction in the 1880s, and that Chauncey
  Hatch Phillips's West Coast Land Company laid out the Shandon town site in 1890; residents dragged
  some buildings over by wagon. Strong card, not written: no fetched page. Try HMDB from another
  fetcher, or Cal Poly Special Collections (West Coast Land Company papers).
- **The name.** Wikipedia says it was named for a town in *Harper's Magazine* in 1891; a search
  snippet says a story "Shandon Bells" in *Harper's New Monthly Magazine*, May 1882. Conflicting and
  unfetched; not used.
- **Population** (1,168 in the 2020 census per Wikipedia) is not in the facts file and was not taken
  from a table; left out.
- The Chapel Hill card's Hearst ceilings rest on Wikipedia alone (confidence would be 3 on that
  sentence; the card's other sentences have two sources).
