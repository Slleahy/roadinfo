# Buttonwillow (town): research notes

Phase P3, writer session 2026-10-04. 3 cards written (cap 8). Before the §9 check.

## Where it plays
`near` is 35.40056, -119.46944, from the Wikipedia infobox. Radius widened to 10,000 m (12,000 m on
the raceway card) rather than the usual 8,000, because the town centre is about 8 km east of the
Interstate 5 / Highway 58 interchange and the cards have to reach a driver on the freeway.

## Cards
- `lone-tree` — the buttonbush the town is named for, the Yokuts meeting place, the stock rodeos,
  the Miller and Lux store, landmark since 1951.
- `buena-vista` — founded 1895 as Buena Vista, cotton, population, the freeway junction.
- `raceway` — Buttonwillow Raceway Park, with `elsewhere` at 35.491306, -119.544944.

The Miller and Lux land empire is **not** in a Buttonwillow card. It is in the tule elk sight card,
which needs the company to explain why Henry Miller is the reason tule elk still exist. Two cards
reciting Miller's acreage would be a duplicate.

## Sources found
- `https://ohp.parks.ca.gov/ListedResources/Detail/492` — the full landmark text, registered
  8 October 1951, located on Buttonwillow Drive half a mile north of Highway 58. This is the best
  source on the stretch: state, short, and it carries the trail, the Yokuts meeting place, the
  rodeos and the Miller and Lux store in three sentences.
- Wikipedia Buttonwillow — Buena Vista 1895, the buttonbush, cotton as the main industry,
  1,337 people at the 2020 census down from 1,508, 269 feet, 26 miles west of Bakersfield.
- Wikipedia Miller & Lux — 1,400,000 acres owned, nearly 22,000 square miles controlled, California's
  largest cattle producer, Miller buying land grants at about $1.15 an acre, the trust dissolving in
  1962.
- Wikipedia Buttonwillow Raceway Park — opened 1995, California Sports Car Club, the 3.000-mile
  23-turn layout, Super Lap Battle since 2004, repaved July 2014, coordinates 35.491306, -119.544944.

## Leads not followed
- **The Lokern hazardous waste facility**, eight miles west, run by Clean Harbors. The Wikipedia
  article says the 1990s expansion fight became an environmental justice case with racial
  discrimination allegations. That is a real card, it would carry `demographics`, and it needs a
  properly fetched court or agency source before anything goes in. Not touched this session.
- **Miller's price per acre** ($1.15) and the 1962 dissolution of the Henry Miller Trust — good
  colour, no card yet.
- **The Best in the West Jet Rally**, an annual model jet meet at Buttonwillow. Unsourced beyond the
  Wikipedia mention.
- Median household income $35,417 and 31.4% of families below the poverty line (2023, from the
  Wikipedia article, not from a table). Not used: the facts file has no place-level income, and
  §6 wants the ACS table. A place-level ACS pull would fix this for every town on the route.
