# Kern National Wildlife Refuge (sight): research notes

Phase P3, writer session 2026-10-04. 1 card (cap 3). Before the §9 check.

## Why only one card, and how it is anchored
The refuge is about ten miles off the route, northeast of Lost Hills, and nothing of it is visible
from Interstate 5. So it is handled the way §4 asks: the card opens with the farmed valley floor the
driver is actually crossing, and the refuge is the elaboration, carrying `{distance}`.

- `near`: 35.61639, -119.69417 (Lost Hills), radius 20,000 m — where the card plays.
- `elsewhere`: 35.74579, -119.6179 — what `{distance}` is measured to.
- The anchor point is the refuge itself with a 25,000 m reach.

A second card was drafted and dropped. Everything left over — the wintering waterfowl, the 8,200
visitors a year, the Pacific Flyway — either needed a "here" opening that no source supported, or
belonged in the valley-floor landscape deck. The San Joaquin Desert line from the refuge's own page
went into the landscape card instead, where it is about ground the driver is on.

## Sources found
- Fish and Wildlife Service About Us — "Kern National Wildlife Refuge was established on
  November 18, 1960", "11,249-acres of natural desert uplands, a relict riparian corridor, and
  developed marsh", "optimum wintering habitat for migratory birds with an emphasis on waterfowl and
  water birds", and "a remnant example of the historic valley uplands in the San Joaquin Desert".
- Fish and Wildlife Service refuge page — 19 miles west of Delano, about 8,200 visitors a year,
  named waterfowl species.
- Wikipedia — the refuge is "situated on the southern margin of what was once the largest freshwater
  wetland complex known as Tulare Lake in the western United States", coordinates 35.74579,
  -119.6179.

## Conflicts
- **Acreage.** The Wikipedia infobox says 1,249 acres; the body text and the Fish and Wildlife
  Service both say 11,249. The card uses 11,249 on the federal page's authority. The infobox is
  almost certainly a dropped digit.
- Distance from Delano is 20 miles on Wikipedia and 19 on the federal page. Neither is in the card.

## Leads not followed
- Where the refuge's water comes from. Neither fetched page says, and for a refuge in a valley this
  dry that is the whole story. A refuge comprehensive conservation plan would have it.
- The bird list and any endangered species on the refuge. The federal species pages were not fetched.
- Waterfowl hunting on a national wildlife refuge is a surprise to most listeners and a fair card,
  with no `sensitive` tag needed under §7 as written.
