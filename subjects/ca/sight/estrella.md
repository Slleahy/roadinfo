# Estrella and the Estrella River: research notes

Phase P4, writer session 2026-10-05. 1 card written (as a sight: the Estrella Adobe Church),
before the §9 check. The river itself is told in the Shandon card
`ca-town-shandon-where-the-estrella-begins-01`.

## Where it is
Estrella Adobe Church, 5660 Airport Road, Paso Robles: 35.680510, -120.640757 (Wikipedia).
LandmarkQuest gives the plaque location as "On Airport Rd, 2.5 mi N of State Hwy 46". `near` radius
6,000 m so it reaches the highway. This is close to the Paso Robles end of the route; the P5 writer
should not duplicate it.

## Card
- estrella-adobe-church: first Protestant church in northern San Luis Obispo County (plaque and
  cemetery district: two sources), built 1879 by settlers on the Estrella Plains, bricks made on site,
  disuse and ruin, rebuilt with more than 5,000 new bricks and rededicated in 1952.

## Gaps
- **Estrella the town**: no Wikipedia article and nothing fetched. The cemetery district mentions a
  Methodist church "in the town of Estrella in 1891", nothing more. No town card.
- **Date of disuse** conflicts: plaque "by 1912 fell into disuse"; cemetery district says a Mennonite
  group used it 1898–1903, then it was left to crumble. The card gives no date.
- The river's length (28.5 miles, Wikipedia) is not a table figure; not used.
- "Estrella" is Spanish for star; no source fetched for the naming. Not used.
