# Lake Nacimiento, Nacimiento River and dam (sight): research notes

Phase P5, writer session 2026-10-05. 2 cards, before the §9 check. Anchored as a point at the
Nacimiento Dam (35.75861111, -120.885, Wikipedia *Nacimiento Dam*), reach and `near` radius
15,000 m. That covers Adelaida and the western end of Nacimiento Lake Drive. The lake is not
claimed to be visible from the route; relevance is scored 3.

## Cards
- dragon-lake: a reservoir on the Nacimiento River northwest of Paso Robles, called Dragon Lake for its
  shape; entirely in SLO County but owned by Monterey County's water agency; built to recharge
  Salinas Valley groundwater; patrolled by Monterey County Parks under a joint powers agreement.
- water-for-paso: the Nacimiento Water Project pipeline, in service in 2011, supplies Paso Robles,
  Templeton, Atascadero and San Luis Obispo; proposed since the 1950s; SLO County's share is small; the
  lake fills fast in wet winters and sits low in droughts.
