# The 2003 San Simeon earthquake in Paso Robles (sight): research notes

Phase P5, writer session 2026-10-05. 2 cards, before the §9 check. Both anchored as a point at
downtown Paso Robles, using the coordinates of the Wikipedia *Paso Robles Hot Springs* article
(35.62555556, -120.69138889, the city-centre spring). `near` radius 5,000 m.

## Cards
- 2003-earthquake: 11:15 am, 22 December 2003; magnitude 6.5; centred in the Santa Lucia Mountains
  near San Simeon (`elsewhere`, 35.62, -121.07, Wikipedia), with `{distance}`; the rupture ran
  southeast toward Paso Robles; two women killed in the 1892 Acorn Building; no retrofitted building
  collapsed; Jenna's Bill. Tagged `tragedy`.
- hole-from-hell: hot spring under the City Hall and library parking lot; sewer-line mistake;
  old bathhouse; "The Hole From Hell"; trench to the Salinas River; filled by 2010.

## Conflicts and weak spots
- **Magnitude**: CISN and Wikipedia *Paso Robles* say 6.5; the quake article says moment magnitude
  6.6. The card uses 6.5.
- **Flow of the spring**: Wikipedia says about 1,300 gallons a minute; AP says 350 gallons a minute
  and 110 degrees. Not used.
- **Pit size**: AP says 80 feet wide and 25 deep; a CalCoastNews search snippet (not fetched) says
  20 feet deep and 100 across. Not used.
- The wrongful-death verdict against the Acorn Building's owner is left out. It is a lawsuit against a
  named private person.
- Second hot spring at the U.S. 101 Paso Robles Street exit: Wikipedia only; not used.
