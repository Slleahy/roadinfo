# Antelope Grade (sight): research notes

Phase P4, writer session 2026-10-05. 1 card written, before the §9 check.

## Where it is
`near` is Polonio Pass, 35.723679, -120.193286 (Wikipedia), radius 8,000 m, which reaches west down
the grade toward Cholame. Wikipedia: Polonio Pass is the eastern approach, Antelope Grade the
western approach; SR 46 rises east from Cholame up the Antelope Grade to a summit near Bluestone
Ridge, then descends through Polonio Pass into the San Joaquin Valley.

## Card
- over-the-coast-ranges: Temblor and Diablo ranges meeting at the pass, rolling grass hills, the
  southernmost crossing of the Diablo Range, Mason's 1912 "easiest pass to the Tulare Lakes", the
  1916 highway bond and paving in December 1930. Dean coming down the grade is in the Dean card.
