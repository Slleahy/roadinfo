# Cameron / Cameron Canyon (sight): research notes

Phase P1, writer session 2026-10-04. **No cards written.** There is not enough sourced material.

## What was looked for
Cameron is a former Southern Pacific timetable station in Tehachapi Pass on the Mojave side, and
the name survives on Cameron Road off State Route 58 and on Cameron Canyon. The batch brief said to
write cards "only if there is real material".

## What was found
Two web searches (free, no page fetches spent) turned up:
- No Wikipedia article for Cameron, Kern County, and no entry on the Office of Historic
  Preservation's Kern County landmark list.
- One substantive claim, from a search summary of railfan material: Cameron Canyon was a trouble
  spot in the steam era, and Southern Pacific train 49, the San Joaquin Flyer, derailed after
  striking a cow on the big curve at the top of Cameron Canyon, in an area railroaders call "the
  Racetrack". No fetched page supports this, and railfan forum recollections are not a reliable
  source for a derailment and a train name.
- Everything else the searches returned was really about the Loop, the pass or Tehachapi itself, all
  of which already have cards.

## Fetch tally
0 of 5 used. Nothing was worth spending a fetch on.

## Leads for a later phase
- The Tehachapi Depot Railroad Museum archive and Southern Pacific employee timetables would settle
  what Cameron actually was: a siding, a helper station, a post office, or all three.
- "The Racetrack" and the derailment at the head of Cameron Canyon is a genuinely good story if a
  reliable source can be found. A newspaper archive search on the San Joaquin Flyer would do it.
- Cameron is also where the oldest turbines in Tehachapi Pass stand, so a sourced Cameron card could
  as easily be about the wind as the railroad. The wind-farm deck is already at its cap of three.
- If nothing better turns up, Cameron is better served by a P7 stretch card on the eastern climb out
  of Mojave than by its own sight deck.
