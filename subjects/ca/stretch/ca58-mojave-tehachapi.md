# CA-58, Mojave to Tehachapi (stretch): research notes

Phase P7, writer session 2026-10-05. 3 cards, before the §9 check. Each direction gets 2 (one
directional card each, plus one bothDirections history card).

## Cards
- `the-pass-and-the-summit` — **Mojave to Tehachapi** (path forward). The pass as connector, the
  Kitanemuk trade route, USGS maps naming the eastern narrows "Tehachapi Pass", the actual high point
  just east of town that the highway sign calls Tehachapi Summit. Directional support: Tehachapi Pass
  article ("narrowest part of the canyon on the eastern approach to the summit") and SR 58 article.
- `not-an-interstate` — **Tehachapi to Mojave** (path reversed). Dropping out of the mountains into
  the Antelope Valley at Mojave, the freeway on past Edwards and Boron, I-5 + SR 58 as the
  Northern California to Las Vegas / I-40 route, submitted for the Interstate system in 1956 and
  1968 and refused.
- `route-466` — bothDirections. US 466 built through the pass in the 1930s; commissioned 25 April
  1933, Morro Bay to Barstow; extended through Las Vegas and over Boulder (Hoover) Dam to Kingman;
  retired in California 1964, gone entirely 1971.
