# CA-58, Bakersfield to Buttonwillow (stretch): research notes

Phase P7, writer session 2026-10-05. 2 cards, one per direction, before the §9 check.

## Route note
The stretch path follows today's signed SR 58: the Westside Parkway, Stockdale Highway, then I-5
north for a few miles to the SR 58 exit at Buttonwillow. Before 2022-2024 SR 58 used Rosedale
Highway instead.

## Cards
- `westside-parkway` — **Bakersfield to Buttonwillow** (path forward). Westside Parkway (finished
  2015, staged from 2013), Kern COG's 1973 call for a west side freeway, the Centennial Corridor
  opening 17 Feb 2024 after displacing dozens of homes and businesses, Rosedale Highway before,
  Stockdale Highway planned as a freeway, the short I-5 overlap.
- `midway-substation` — **Buttonwillow to Bakersfield** (path reversed). Midway Substation a mile
  east of Buttonwillow, where PG&E's Path 15 lines from Los Banos meet SCE's Path 26 lines over the
  Tehachapis; Path 26 as the crucial link between the two grids. Written for the eastbound driver
  because the substation is at the start of that direction and ties to the mountains ahead.
