# I-5, Buttonwillow to Lost Hills (stretch): research notes

Phase P7, writer session 2026-10-05. 2 cards, before the §9 check.

## Cards
- `signs-for-san-francisco` — **Buttonwillow to Lost Hills** (northbound, path forward). The West
  Side Freeway, the 1956 choice between converting US 99 and a new west-side route that bypassed all
  the valley towns, the freeway also bypassing the Bay Area while northbound signs still list
  San Francisco as the control city ("on northbound I-5 between SR 99 and I-580").
- `long-and-lonely` — **bothDirections**. Opened 1972 (second phase, south to Wheeler Ridge), "a long
  and lonely route with no businesses alongside", cars running out of fuel, the median wide enough
  for six or eight lanes.

## The direction problem
The view does not differ much between northbound and southbound here, and no source was found for
anything southbound-specific (the southbound control city is not stated in the article). The
history card is therefore honestly marked bothDirections, which leaves the drive home with one card
on this leg, not two. Candidate southbound material, unsourced: southbound signage; the Path 15
lines (already used at Midway on the CA-58 stretch, so not repeated here).
