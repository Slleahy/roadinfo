# CA-58, Tehachapi to Bakersfield (stretch): research notes

Phase P7, writer session 2026-10-05. 2 cards, one per direction, before the §9 check.

## Cards
- `old-466-down-the-creek` — **Tehachapi to Bakersfield** (path forward). Old US 466 west of
  Tehachapi on what is now Woodford-Tehachapi Road, on a cliff above the railroad, past the Loop,
  through Keene, then Bena Road down Caliente Creek into the valley and through Edison; not rebuilt
  as expressway/freeway until it became Route 58. The source describes the route westward.
- `truck-climbing-lane` — **Bakersfield to Tehachapi** (path reversed). Two lanes each way from
  Caliente to Keene, slow trucks on the climb, Caltrans's "most critical section", the planned
  eastbound truck climbing lane and repaving with wildlife undercrossings, three segments over
  three years likely from 2028 (Tehachapi News, 9 Aug 2026, via cahighways). `asOf` 2026.
