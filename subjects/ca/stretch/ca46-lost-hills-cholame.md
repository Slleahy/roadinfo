# CA-46, Lost Hills to Cholame (stretch): research notes

Phase P7, writer session 2026-10-05. 2 cards, one per direction, before the §9 check.

## Cards
- `blackwells-corner` — **Lost Hills to Cholame** (path forward). The straight road to Blackwells
  Corner and SR 33; George Blackwell's 1921 rest stop; James Dean's brief stop there on 30 Sept 1955,
  meeting two fellow racers bound for Salinas; leaving about 5:15 p.m. west on US 466 and cresting
  Polonio Pass. Tagged `tragedy`. Does not repeat the crash itself (sight card last-drive).
- `antelope-grade-climb` — **Cholame to Lost Hills** (path reversed). Climbing east up the Antelope
  Grade to a summit near Bluestone Ridge and down through Polonio Pass (SR 46 article, written
  eastward); the 1959 straightening that abandoned the original Antelope Grade road; the steep grade
  slowing trucks; the planned four-lane expressway on a new line to the north, closing the gap in a
  continuous expressway from I-5 to US 101; construction expected spring 2027 (Paso Robles Daily
  News, 13 June 2025). This is the widening left for P7 in the Antelope Grade notes. `asOf` 2025.

## Conflicts and weak spots
- **Start of construction:** Wikipedia SR 46 says the Antelope Grade Section is "scheduled to begin
  construction in 2026"; Paso Robles Daily News (June 2025) via cahighways says spring 2027; KSBY
  (Oct 2023) said 2027. The card says "as of mid-2025 ... spring of 2027".
- "Most volume of trucks on the central coast" (KSBY quoting Caltrans): single-source superlative;
  left out, the card says only that the grade slows trucks.
- Blackwells Corner as "the last place James Dean was seen alive" (Wikipedia): imprecise, since
  witnesses saw him at the crash; not used.
- "The racer's road" nickname for SR 33: disputed in the source (Beath); not used.
- Fellow racers Reventlow and Kessler are not named.
