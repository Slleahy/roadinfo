# CA-46, Cholame to Paso Robles (stretch): research notes

Phase P7, writer session 2026-10-05. 2 cards, one per direction, before the §9 check.

## Cards
- `route-466-moves` — **Cholame to Paso Robles** (path forward). US 466 followed this road only as
  far as Shandon until 1958, then turned toward Atascadero and Morro Bay on today's SR 41; that road
  was unpaved/substandard, while Shandon–Paso Robles (then Sign Route 41) had been improved and was
  the favored road; in 1964 this became SR 46 and the old route became SR 41.
- `widening-blood-alley` — **Paso Robles to Cholame** (path reversed). East from Paso Robles past
  Whitley Gardens and alongside the Estrella River (SR 46 article, written eastward); Blood Alley and
  its head-on crashes; the December 1995 interim fix (daytime headlights, barrier, rumble strips);
  widening from 2008/2010 moving east, reaching Shandon–Cholame in 2022. Tagged `tragedy`. Avoids the
  Wye card's material (junction name, 1958 layout, 2025 overpass).

## Conflicts and weak spots
- Wikipedia says LRN 125 "had still not been paved between Cholame and Atascadero by the 1950s";

  The card says "still not paved", citing Wikipedia.
- Crash and death counts (206 accidents and 17 deaths 1994–1999; 38 deaths 2000–2010) are prose,
  not a table; left out under §6.
- Truck share of traffic (40% of AADT) is prose; left out.
