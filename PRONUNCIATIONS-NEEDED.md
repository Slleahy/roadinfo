# Pronunciations needed

Written by `scripts/scan_pronunciations.py`. Every word here is one the narrator
voice has to guess at, and guesses are where mangled names come from.

Workflow: add the ones you are sure of to the app's `RoadSavant/Resources/Pronunciations.json`,
then hand what is left to the owner, who will find or phone someone who says the word.
Never invent a pronunciation. If a word cannot be sourced, rewrite the sentence without it.
